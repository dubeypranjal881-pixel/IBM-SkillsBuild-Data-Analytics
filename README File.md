## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end data analytics and predictive modeling study on how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automation impact modern job roles across various industries. By analyzing workforce trends, risk factors, income disparities, and key performance metrics, this analysis identifies occupations most vulnerable to AI disruption and provides actionable insights for reskilling and workforce planning.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
- **Dataset File:** `ai-impact-jobs-layoff-risk-dataset.csv`
- **Focus Areas:**
  - AI Work Automation Risk Score ($0 - 100\%$)
  - Industry Sectors & Job Roles
  - Salary & Skill Adaptability Index
  - Historical & Projected Layoff Metrics
  - Experience Levels & Educational Backgrounds

- **Development Environment:** Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

---

## 🔑 Key Insights & Findings

1. **High-Risk Sectors:** Administrative, Routine Data Entry, and Basic Support Services show the highest vulnerability ($>75\%$) to AI automation.
2. **Reskilling & Adaptability Correlation:** Job roles with high Skill Adaptability Indexes show significantly lower layoff risks despite high AI integration.
3. **Salary & Automation:** Entry to mid-level analytical and routine technical roles face near-term restructuring, whereas human-centric and strategic roles retain high stability.
